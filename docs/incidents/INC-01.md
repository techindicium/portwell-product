# INC-01: a superseded refund window reached a customer

**Written:** 2026-07-14, three days after the fact, by whoever was on call.
**Severity:** the account disputed an invoice. No money moved.

## What happened

An Assist proposal answering a billing question quoted a 30-day refund window. The current
window is 60 days. The proposal was sent without a human reading it.

The 30-day figure came from a published knowledge article. That article had been superseded
months earlier by a newer one carrying the correct window. Both were published. Both were
retrievable. Nothing distinguished them at the point of retrieval.

## What was checked before it went out

The article was published. That was the whole check.

## What nobody checked

Whether the article had been superseded. The newer article names the older one as the article it
replaces, so the relationship was recorded. Nothing read it.

## What was done afterwards

The account was called. The older article's status was changed by hand. Nobody wrote down who
changed it or when, so the change is not in this record either.

## What would have caught it

Unclear, and this is the honest part of the writeup. Candidates that came up at the time:

- Not retrieving an article that another article supersedes.
- Requiring a human on billing answers for this account's tier, which a policy already says.
- Noticing that two retrieved articles gave different answers to the same question.

None of the three existed. None of the three exists now.

## Open

Nobody owns the follow-up. It was discussed in a retrospective and not written into any tracker.
